Our team

How we play

2-5-1 is our starting shape — not a cage. Once the whistle goes, the game doesn't stand still, so neither do we. We're a fluid team: you read what's in front of you, make your move, and trust the players around you to cover the gap you leave behind. Your position is where you start, not where you're stuck.

Our base 2-5-1 shape — two centre backs, five across midfield, one striker.
CB steps into midfield?The nearest midfielder drops in behind to cover — the back line stays whole.
Wide player pushes on?The nearest teammate shifts across to cover the space in behind.
Someone presses?The nearest teammate adjusts alongside them to keep our shape balanced.
Space opens ahead?Go and take it — join the attack. A teammate stays alert to the gap you leave, so we're never caught out.
Team rules that never change: hunt in pairs, always give a passing option, and go through, round or over — in that order.
Someone covers, someone fills in — the shape stays whole even as players move. Read it, decide, commit, and trust a teammate to cover you. Being brave and thinking for yourself matters more than standing in a fixed spot.

In and out of possession

Your role in the team

This is your job in open play — not set pieces. What you do with the ball, and without it, every matchday.

GKGoalkeeperStarts our attacks
In possession
You start our attacks. Stay calm on the ball, scan before it reaches you, and be brave enough to play short and keep possession. You're the spare player — always available for a pass back to switch the play.
Out of possession
Read the game in front of you. Push up to the edge of your box when we're high so nothing gets in behind, drop when we're pinned back. Command your box and organise the players ahead of you.
Your job in the team
Be the calm head that starts us playing out and keeps things steady behind. Alert, composed, always watching the whole picture.
CBCentre BacksLCB & RCB
In possession
Split wide to give us width at the back and receive side-on so your first touch goes forward. If nobody presses you, drive into midfield with the ball — but only if a midfielder drops to fill the gap behind you.
Out of possession
Stay connected as a pair — when one steps out to challenge, the other covers behind. Defend the space in behind first, win your headers, and don't dive in. Push the line up when we win it high.
Your job in the team
Be our foundation. Brave on the ball, organised without it, and always talking to each other so we're never both caught out.
CMDeep CMThe link player
In possession
Stay central and drop in front of the centre backs to pick the ball up from them and link us together. Keep moving to offer angles, receive on the half-turn, and always look forward first. You give us the spare man that beats their press.
Out of possession
Screen the space in front of the back two — cut out the pass into their strikers. Be the first to drop into any gap a centre back leaves when they step up.
Your job in the team
Be the calm link that keeps us connected. Rarely spectacular, always important — the player who makes everyone around them tidier.
CMAdvanced CMsThe two higher CMs
In possession
Find the pockets of space between their midfield and defence. Stay on different lines to each other, check your shoulder before the ball comes, and receive facing their goal so you can drive or slide a runner in.
Out of possession
Press with purpose — when one of you jumps, the other tucks in to cover. Win the ball high up the pitch and get us going the other way fast.
Your job in the team
Be our engine — box to box, goals and assists at one end, tackles and pressure at the other. Read whether to support the attack or protect the middle, and make that choice yourself.
LMWide MidfieldersLM & RM
In possession
Hold your width and stretch the pitch — stay high and wide until the ball's ready to come, then attack the space. When we build on your side, be an option; when it's on the far side, come inside to give us a target and threaten the back post.
Out of possession
Track back to help your defence and stay alert to press their wide players. If a centre back or midfielder gets dragged out, be ready to shuttle across and cover the space.
Your job in the team
Be our width and our threat — the players who make the pitch big when we attack and get us up the pitch fast on the break.
CFCentre ForwardStays high
In possession
Stay high and make yourself hard to mark — position between their centre backs, or drift across so they can't pick you up easily. Stretch them with runs into the channels, hold the ball up when it comes long, and be the out-ball when we're under pressure.
Out of possession
Lead the press — curve your run to force them one way, and never press alone; the nearest midfielder goes with you. Set the trap that wins us the ball high.
Your job in the team
Be our point of the spear — the runner who stretches them, the presser who starts our defending, and the finisher who makes it count.

If you remember nothing else

Three team rules

1
Hunt in pairs

Nobody presses alone. The nearest teammate always goes with the first presser.

2
Always make an option

Whoever's on the ball, everyone else keeps moving to offer a safe pass — never standing still waiting to be found. This is every player, every time we have the ball, not just at restarts.

3
Through, round, or over

Go through the middle if it's on — that's always first choice. If it's shut, go round them wide down the flanks. If both are blocked, go over the top with a long pass or a through ball in behind.
