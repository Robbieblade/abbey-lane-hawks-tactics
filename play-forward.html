If you remember nothing else

The ten rules

Part 1

Win it high

Our attacks start without the ball. Win it in their half and we’re already close to goal. Then receive it so you can play forward straight away.

Rule 1

Curve your press

NORun straight at him. He passes round you.
YESCurve your run. Block the pass to his mate.

Curve your press

Run straight and he just passes to the player beside him. Curve it so your body sits between him and that pass. Now he has to go long or give it away.

Who: CF starts it. The nearest midfielders squeeze up behind so the long ball is ours too. Hunt in pairs.

Rule 2

Open your body

NOSquare on, back to goal. You get caught.
YESCheck your shoulder. Open up. Face forward.

Scan first, open your body

Look over your shoulder before the ball arrives, so you know where the defender is. Open your body and take it on your back foot. One touch and you’re facing the whole pitch.

Who: all five midfielders, and CBs receiving from Josh.

Part 2

Movement

The player on the ball can only play forward if someone has moved. Six ways to make the next pass possible.

Rule 3

Pass, then move

NOPass and stand still. Your teammate is stuck.
YESPass and run. Two options, one defender.

Pass, then move

Stand and watch and the defender steps in front of your teammate. Move straight away and the defender has to choose between you. He can’t cover both.

Who: everyone, especially the midfield five making triangles.

Rule 4

Pass to space, not feet

NOBall to his feet. The defender steps in.
YESBall into space. He runs onto it.

Space, not feet

When a teammate is running, play it into the space in front of him. Ball and runner arrive together, and the defender has to turn and chase.

Who: CBs and central mids. See the run first, then pick the space.

Rule 5

Get on the blind side

NOStand where he can see you.
YESHide behind his shoulder. He looks away, go.

Hide, then go

You’re not too slow to score, you’re too easy to see. Start behind the defender’s shoulder. The moment he turns his head to watch the ball, go. Thomas Müller called himself a “space interpreter” and set a Bundesliga assist record doing this.

Who: CF, and any midfielder arriving late in the box.

Rule 6

Start deep, arrive late

NOStart level with him. Offside.
YESStart deep. Go when the passer looks up.

Start deep, arrive late

Hang back, then go when the passer lifts his head. You hit the line at full speed while the defender is still turning, and you’re onside.

Who: CF and wide mids. Watch the passer’s head, not the ball.

Rule 7

Overlap on the outside

NOStay behind the winger. He’s stuck.
YESSprint round the outside. He can’t stop both.

He cuts in, you go outside

The clip shows a full-back. We play 2-5-1, so the run comes from the nearest central mid. A CB only goes if the other CB and a midfielder are covering. Wide mid: when he overlaps, cut inside and play him in or use the space.

Who: advanced CMs and wide mids working together.

Rule 8

Switch the play

NOPlay into the crowd. You get squeezed.
YESSwitch it. Our wide mid gets space.

Busy one side? Play the other

When one side is packed, their whole defence has slid across. Switch it long and our far-side wide mid has space to run into. You only see it if you’ve scanned (Rule 2).

Who: far-side wide mid stays wide and ready. Centre mids scan both sides.

Part 3

Finish it

All that work needs a clean strike at the end. And the same idea from the other side: how Josh makes the goal smaller.

Rule 9

Get over the ball

NOLean back. It flies over the bar.
YESPlant beside it. Chest over the ball.

Chest over the ball

Three steps: plant your foot beside the ball, not behind it. Knee and chest over the ball. Laces through the middle and land on your kicking foot. Frank Lampard scored 177 Premier League goals from midfield like this.

Who: everyone.

Rule 10 · Goalkeeper

Close the angle

NOStay on your line. Half the goal is open.
YESStep out about 5 metres. The goal looks tiny.

Close the distance, close the angle

Your reach doesn’t change, but stepping out makes it cover far more of the goal from the striker’s view. Outfielders, flip it: if their keeper rushes out, shoot early or go round.

Who: Josh. More on the Goalkeeping page.

Test yourself

What do you do?

Answer in your head first, then tap to check.

Their centre-back has the ball. How do you press him?

Curve your run so your body blocks the pass to his teammate. He has to go long or give it away. (Rule 1)

The ball’s coming to you in midfield. What do you do before it arrives?

Check your shoulder and open your body, so your first touch takes you forward. (Rule 2)

You’ve just passed. What now?

Move straight away. Give your teammate two options. (Rule 3)

Our side is packed with their players. Where does the ball go?

Switch it to the other side, where our wide mid is waiting. (Rule 8)

When does the striker start his run?

From deep, the moment the passer looks up. (Rule 6)

Josh, it’s one-on-one. Stay on your line or step out?

Step out to close the distance and the angle. (Rule 10)
