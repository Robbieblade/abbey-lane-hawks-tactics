Throw-ins — keep it, don't gift it

A throw is possession handed to us. Four options, every time. The thrower scans before they pick the ball up, and we never throw into a crowd we can't win.

Four options: down the line, set back, third man, or switch away. The nearest CB gives safety behind.

What the thrower looks for — in order

1Down the line — CF or wide midfielder pulling wide into space to run at them
2Set back — a CM checking in to feet, laying it back first time
3Third man — set-back player flicks it to a runner arriving beyond
4Switch away — if the near side's crowded, move it to the far wide midfielder in space

Rules for everyone

  • Receivers show at different distances — one short, one long — never all at the same depth.
  • Check your shoulder before the ball arrives so you know where the space is.
  • Always keep a man behind the ball (nearest CB) so a loose one doesn't get countered.
  • If in doubt, take the safe option and keep possession — we build again.
