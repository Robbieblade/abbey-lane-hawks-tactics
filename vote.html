September winners

September Player of the Month

Our first winners of the season — voted by the supporters, the players, and chosen by the coaches.

Supporters' Player

Logan Brown

Supporters picked Logan for his attitude and positivity — always lifting the team, praising good play and keeping spirits up when things aren't going well. He kept his composure when an opponent tried to wind him up, and chipped in with some brilliant goals and quality from corners too.

Players' Player

Osca Thompson

The players chose Osca for his relentless work rate and the way he leads. Teammates said he's never afraid to put a challenge in, wins everything, is solid at the back and keeps his head — a captain full of encouragement who lifts everyone around him.

Coaches' Player

Zac McGarry

Chosen by the coaches for always putting in full effort, following instructions and doing whatever the team needs — including playing multiple positions without complaint — while still delivering real quality with goals and assists.
